Summary

Fibroblasts from four individuals were reprogrammed to induced pluripotent stem cells and sampled at up to 11 time points. Cells were profiled for single-nucleus gene expression and chromatin accessibility (10x Multiome), DNA methylation with transcriptome (snMCT-seq), and DNA methylation with 3D genome organization (snM3C-seq). Highly multiplexed spatial transcriptomics (CosMx) was performed on selected time points, and each donor was whole-genome sequenced.
